The Architectural Shift

Continuous Bidirectional Interaction

Why turn-based pipelines break conversational realism, and how duplex architectures target human latency budgets.

Legacy Cascaded Pipeline

Audio In → STT → Silence Timeout (700ms) → LLM → TTS → Audio Out

Latency compounds across each independent stage. The mandatory silence detection timeout is often longer than natural turn-taking thresholds (200-300ms), making the bot feel unresponsive and robotic.

Native Full-Duplex Engine

Audio In ⇔ Unified Multimodal Transformer ⇔ Audio Out (Target: Every 80ms)

The model receives raw audio tokens continuously. It autonomously predicts whether to speak, listen, backchannel (e.g., "haan", "okay"), or gracefully pause mid-syllable when interrupted.

Three Execution Levers

Our Optimization Architecture

LEVER 01 · RUNTIME

Batched Graph Execution

Cross-session tensor batching under unified 80ms ticks. CUDA graphs locked to static shapes eliminate kernel launch gaps. Attention sink sliding-windows prevent unbounded memory growth.

LEVER 02 · TELEPHONY

8kHz Channel Adaptation

Most foundation models train on 24kHz wideband audio. We evaluate acoustic degradation over Indian narrowband telecom networks (AMR / G.711) and deploy lightweight pre-model normalizers.

LEVER 03 · INDIC NLP

Language & Accent Adapters

Building atop open Hindi baselines (Human-1) to support Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, and conversational Hinglish code-mixing using synthetic dual-channel dialogue datasets.
