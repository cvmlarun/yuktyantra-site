Internships for B.Tech and B.Sc Horticulture

YuktYantra provides structured, mentored undergraduate research internships and capstone fellowships for students pursuing B.Tech and B.Sc Horticulture degrees. Work on physical perception, agricultural camera carts, and conversational speech models.

Academic Year 2026–2027 · CSE · ECE · AI/ML · ISE · Mechanical · Horticulture · Agriculture

Real industrial data, clear evaluation, professional mentorship

Undergraduate students rarely get to work on real, uncurated physical and agronomic data before graduation. Our internship programme bridges that gap.

  4. Cohort Kickoff: Repository access, initial dataset samples, and bi-weekly milestone roadmap issued.
Curriculum & Capstones

Three Structured Internship Tracks

Each project track maps onto an ongoing YuktYantra applied research initiative with defined student deliverables.

TRACK 01 · EMBODIED VISION & POSE (B.TECH)

Mobile Challenge Pose & Success Rater

Implement on-device computer vision models to score camera-based household manipulation challenges: Steady Stack (tumbler verticality and wobble), Dal Sort (bean bounding boxes and bowl count purity), and Fold Off (cloth edge parallelism).

SKILLS & DELIVERABLES
  • • Hand pose estimation (MediaPipe / custom PyTorch)
  • • End-state segmentation & scoring heuristic design
  • • Benchmark validation dataset with failure modes logged
  • • Recommended for: B.Tech CSE, ISE, AI/ML students
TRACK 02 · FIELD IMAGING (B.TECH / B.SC HORTICULTURE)

Viticulture Smartphone Imaging Cart & Field Survey

Assemble and operate a lightweight manual push-cart rig using mounted smartphones across vineyard rows in Kudari Salawadgi, Basavana Bagewadi taluk. Record video passes and collect physical hand measurements (berry sizing with vernier calipers and refractometer sugar readings) across phenological stages to build an initial open dataset.

SKILLS & DELIVERABLES
  • • Smartphone video acquisition & spatial row indexing
  • • Hand physiological measurements (caliper diameter & Brix refractometer)
  • • Scheduled field visits in Kudari Salawadgi (near Huvina Hippargi), Basavana Bagewadi taluk (Vijayapura district)
  • • Recommended for: B.Sc Horticulture, B.Tech Agriculture, ECE, Mechanical, CSE
TRACK 03 · SPEECH & TELEPHONY (B.TECH) When Compute Available

Full-Duplex Speech & Indic Telephony Adapters

Profile open full-duplex speech foundation models (PersonaPlex, Human-1) for Indian telephony. Full-scale GPU profiling and model fine-tuning will run when GPU grant allocations or compute sponsorship are activated. Current prep work focuses on CPU inference tracing, 8 kHz narrowband audio normalizers, and synthetic Kannada dialogue dataset curation.

SKILLS & DELIVERABLES
  • • PyTorch CPU inference profiling & execution tracing
  • • 8kHz vs 24kHz audio tokenization quality benchmarks
  • • Synthetic two-channel conversational Kannada dataset curation
  • • Note: GPU benchmarking scheduled upon compute cluster allocation

FIELD SAFETY & LOGISTICS

  • • Location: Field visits take place at our private research vineyard block in Kudari Salawadgi (near Huvina Hippargi), Basavana Bagewadi taluk, Vijayapura district (exact directions provided upon selection).
