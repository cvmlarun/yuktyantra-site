Crowdsourced Physical AI

The Mobile "Skill Challenge" Data Engine

Niantic used mobile player scans to assemble geospatial models. We adapt this crowdsourcing paradigm to domestic robotic manipulation: mobile skill challenges where users attempt physical dexterity tasks and on-device computer vision grades each attempt.

The Breakthrough: The Rater is the Reward Model

A neural vision model that scores "did they fold the shirt cleanly / stack the tumblers stably / tie the bowline correctly" is fundamentally a success detector—identical to an RL reward function. Every attempt yields dense, labelled trajectory data that passive video scrapers can never provide.

Structured Domestic Tasks over Random Motion

Instead of front-camera fitness jumping jacks, our challenge specifications feature propped-phone or headband egocentric angles capturing close-up hand-object physics: deformable dough, cloth, micro-beans, and fluid meniscus alignment.

Phase 1 & Phase 2 Challenge Portfolio

10 Standardized Camera-Rated Manipulation Challenge Concepts

Scored primarily from end-state verification plus temporal hand-pose trajectories. Phase 1 targets (Steady Stack, Dal Sort, Fold Off) form the foundation for undergraduate student vision raters.

CHALLENGE 01 Phase 1 Target

Steady Stack

Stack steel tumblers, brass cups, or coins as high as possible against a 30s timer.

End-State Rater: Stack height, vertical plumb line, wobble delta.
Data Target: Precise placement and stability.
CHALLENGE 02 Phase 1 Target

Dal Sort

Separate mixed rajma, chana, and moong beans into three distinct bowls.

End-State Rater: Purity ratio per bowl, sorting speed.
Data Target: High-volume pick-and-place with varied pinches.
CHALLENGE 03 Phase 1 Target

Fold Off

Fold a cotton T-shirt or towel into a compact rectangle against time.

End-State Rater: Edge parallelism, symmetry, footprint.
Data Target: Deformable 2D cloth manipulation benchmark.
CHALLENGE 04 Concept

Roti Round

Roll dough into a uniform circle using a traditional rolling pin (belan).

End-State Rater: Boundary circularity, thickness variance.
Data Target: Deformable elastoplastic tool control.
CHALLENGE 05 Concept

Knot Master

Tie a specified knot (shoelace, reef knot, or bowline) shown on screen.

End-State Rater: Topological knot graph classification.
Data Target: Fine bimanual dexterity and cord tracing.
CHALLENGE 06 Concept

Pour Perfect

Pour water from a container into a glass precisely to a marked indicator line.

End-State Rater: Meniscus fill line delta, spill count.
Data Target: Liquid handling and wrist rotational torque.
Compliance & Governance

Privacy Architecture Designed Around India's DPDP Act

Collecting visual data from homes requires ethical safeguards and privacy preservation. YuktYantra specifies on-device sanitization before any video chunk ever leaves the participant's device.

ON-DEVICE BLURRING

Local Face & Identity Masking

A lightweight edge model detects and blurs human faces, personal photos, and background identity markers directly in device RAM before video encoding.

AGE & CONSENT GATE

Strict 18+ Verifiable Opt-In

Explicit digital consent flow designed around DPDP requirements. Zero collection from minors, with encrypted, revocable contributor consent logs.

BANDWIDTH & SELECTION

Selective Wi-Fi Sync

Only scored challenge episodes are preserved. High-score attempts and informative failure trajectories are queued locally and synced over Wi-Fi.
